# Lesson Plan Framework

## Structure Lessons by Skill Level

1. **Beginner**
   - Basic concepts and operations
   - Simple scenarios
2. **Intermediate**
   - Advanced concepts and operations
   - Complex scenarios
3. **Advanced**
   - Expert-level concepts and operations
   - Real-world scenarios

## Map to Simulator Scenarios

- **Beginner**
  - Scenario 1: Takeoff and landing
  - Scenario 2: Basic navigation
- **Intermediate**
  - Scenario 3: Weather interpretation
  - Scenario 4: Emergency procedures
- **Advanced**
  - Scenario 5: Instrument flying
  - Scenario 6: Air traffic control interactions

## Define Completion Criteria

- **Beginner**
  - Successfully complete Scenario 1 and Scenario 2 with minimal errors
- **Intermediate**
  - Successfully complete Scenario 3 and Scenario 4 with minimal errors
- **Advanced**
  - Successfully complete Scenario 5 and Scenario 6 with minimal errors
