# Story: Envelope protection and limits

## Bank Angle Limits
- Normal: ±25°
- Approach: ±20°
- Low speed: reduced to maintain coordinated turns

## Pitch Limits
- Nose up: +20° (stall prevention)
- Nose down: -15° (overspeed prevention)

## Protections
- Overspeed protection (pitch up near Vne)
- Stall protection (pitch down near critical AOA)
