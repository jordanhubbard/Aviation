# Privacy and Data Retention Rules

## Personal Identifiable Information (PII) Handling

- **Data Collected**: The applications collect the following PII data:
  - Names
  - Email addresses
  - Contact numbers
  - Flight logs
  - User account information
- **Data Processing**: PII is processed in compliance with relevant regulations such as GDPR and CCPA.
- **Data Storage**: PII is stored securely using encrypted databases and is retained only for as long as necessary.

## Retention and Deletion Policies

- **Retention Period**: The retention period for PII data is as follows:
  - Names and contact numbers: 7 years from the date of last interaction.
  - Email addresses: 2 years from the date of last interaction.
  - Flight logs: 10 years from the date of the flight.
  - User account information: 15 years from the date of account closure.
- **Deletion Process**: A secure deletion process is implemented to remove data once the retention period has expired. This includes anonymizing data before deletion and ensuring that backups are also deleted.

## Consent Requirements

- **User Consent**: Explicit consent is obtained from users before collecting their personal data through clear and concise consent forms.
- **Consent Management**: Records of user consent are maintained in a dedicated database, and mechanisms are provided for users to withdraw consent at any time. Users can request data deletion or modification via a dedicated support channel.

## Additional Notes

- **Compliance Monitoring**: Regular audits are conducted to ensure compliance with privacy laws and regulations.
- **Data Minimization**: We strive to collect only the minimum amount of PII necessary for our operations.
- **Access Control**: Access to PII data is restricted to authorized personnel only.
- **Incident Response**: We have established procedures to respond to data breaches or unauthorized access incidents promptly.
- **Training**: Employees receive regular training on privacy policies and data protection best practices.
- **Third-Party Vendors**: Third-party vendors are required to comply with our privacy policies and undergo regular security assessments.
- **Data Subject Rights**: Users can exercise their rights under GDPR and CCPA, including the right to access, correct, and delete their data.
- **Data Transfer**: Any transfer of PII data to third parties is conducted securely and in compliance with applicable laws.
- **Data Encryption**: All PII data is encrypted both at rest and in transit.
- **Data Backup**: Regular backups of PII data are encrypted and stored securely offsite.
- **Data Disposal**: Physical media containing PII data is disposed of securely to prevent unauthorized access.
