# Data Licensing Review

## Overview
This document reviews the licensing of various data sources used across the Aviation monorepo applications, including flight data, weather data, and charts.

## Data Sources and Licenses

### Flight Data
- **Source:** FlightAware, AviationStack
- **License:** Proprietary
- **Attribution Requirements:** Must credit FlightAware and AviationStack in any public displays of data.
- **Restrictions:** Non-redistribution without explicit permission.

### Weather Data
- **Source:** AviationWeather.gov, OpenWeatherMap
- **License:** Public Domain (AviationWeather.gov), Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (OpenWeatherMap)
- **Attribution Requirements:** Must credit AviationWeather.gov and OpenWeatherMap in any public displays of data.
- **Restrictions:** Redistribution allowed under the terms of the OpenWeatherMap license.

### Charts and Maps
- **Source:** OurAirports, OpenAIP
- **License:** Public Domain (OurAirports), Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 (OpenAIP)
- **Attribution Requirements:** Must credit OurAirports and OpenAIP in any public displays of data.
- **Restrictions:** Redistribution allowed under the terms of the ODbL license.

## Attribution Requirements
- **Flight Data:** Credit FlightAware and AviationStack.
- **Weather Data:** Credit AviationWeather.gov and OpenWeatherMap.
- **Charts and Maps:** Credit OurAirports and OpenAIP.

## Restrictions for Redistribution
- **Flight Data:** Non-redistribution without explicit permission.
- **Weather Data:** Redistribution allowed under the terms of the OpenWeatherMap license.
- **Charts and Maps:** Redistribution allowed under the terms of the ODbL license.

## Summary
This review identifies the data sources used in the Aviation monorepo, their respective licenses, attribution requirements, and restrictions for redistribution. Compliance with these guidelines ensures legal and ethical use of the data.
