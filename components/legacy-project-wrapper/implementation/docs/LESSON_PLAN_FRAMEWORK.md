# Lesson Plan Framework

## Overview
This document outlines the framework for defining lesson plans to enhance educational content within the aviation applications.

## Structure Lessons by Skill Level
Lessons will be structured based on the following skill levels:

1. **Beginner**: Introduces fundamental concepts and basic operations.
2. **Intermediate**: Builds on foundational knowledge with more complex tasks.
3. **Advanced**: Focuses on advanced topics and real-world applications.

## Mapping to Simulator Scenarios
Each lesson will be mapped to specific simulator scenarios to provide practical, hands-on experience:

- **Scenario 1**: Basic Takeoff and Landing
- **Scenario 2**: Navigation and Waypoint Planning
- **Scenario 3**: Emergency Procedures

## Completion Criteria
To ensure learners have mastered the material, the following completion criteria will be defined for each lesson:

- **Demonstration**: Successfully perform the required tasks in the simulator.
- **Quiz**: Answer a set of questions related to the lesson content.
- **Review**: Provide a written summary or reflection on the lesson.

## Example Lesson Plan

### Title: Basic Takeoff and Landing

#### Skill Level: Beginner

#### Simulator Scenario: Basic Takeoff and Landing

#### Objectives:
- Understand pre-flight checks.
- Perform a safe takeoff procedure.
- Execute a stable landing.

#### Completion Criteria:
- Demonstrate the ability to perform a takeoff and landing in the simulator.
- Score at least 80% on the post-lesson quiz.
- Submit a written reflection on the lesson.

## Conclusion
This lesson plan framework ensures a structured and effective learning experience, tailored to different skill levels and reinforced through practical application and assessment.
