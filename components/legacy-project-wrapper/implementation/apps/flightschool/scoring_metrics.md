# Learner Scoring Metrics

## Accuracy Metrics
- **Metric 1: Accuracy Rate**
  - Definition: Percentage of correct answers provided by the learner.
  - Calculation: (Number of Correct Answers / Total Number of Questions) * 100
- **Metric 2: Precision**
  - Definition: Ratio of correctly identified positive observations to the total predicted positives.
  - Calculation: (True Positives / (True Positives + False Positives))

## Timing Metrics
- **Metric 3: Response Time**
  - Definition: Average time taken by the learner to respond to questions or tasks.
  - Calculation: Sum of Response Times / Total Number of Responses
- **Metric 4: Task Completion Time**
  - Definition: Time taken to complete a given task or scenario.
  - Calculation: End Time - Start Time

## Safety Metrics
- **Metric 5: Safety Violations**
  - Definition: Number of safety rules violated during the simulation.
  - Calculation: Count of Safety Violations
- **Metric 6: Incident Rate**
  - Definition: Frequency of incidents occurring during the simulation.
  - Calculation: (Number of Incidents / Total Number of Simulations) * 100

## Scoring Rubric
| Metric          | Weight | Description                                                                 |
|-----------------|--------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Accuracy Rate   | 40%    | Percentage of correct answers provided by the learner.                      |
| Precision       | 30%    | Ratio of correctly identified positive observations to the total predicted positives.|
| Response Time   | 10%    | Average time taken by the learner to respond to questions or tasks.         |
| Task Completion Time | 10%  | Time taken to complete a given task or scenario.                            |
| Safety Violations | 5%     | Number of safety rules violated during the simulation.                      |
| Incident Rate   | 5%     | Frequency of incidents occurring during the simulation.                     |
