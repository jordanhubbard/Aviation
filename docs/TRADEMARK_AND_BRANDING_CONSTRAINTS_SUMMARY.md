# Trademark and Branding Constraints Summary

This document provides a summary of the trademark and branding constraints for references to Garmin® and G1000® within the Aviation project.

## Key Constraints

### Trademark Usage Guidance
- **Prohibited Uses:**
  - Do not use Garmin logos or trademarks in a way that implies sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation without explicit permission.
- **Permitted Uses:**
  - Use Garmin and G1000 names/logos for educational and training purposes only, with clear disclaimers.

### Acceptable Naming and Disclaimers
- **Naming Conventions:**
  - Avoid using "Garmin" or "G1000" in product names.
  - Use terms like "Garmin-style" or "G1000-like" to indicate similarity.
- **Disclaimers:**
  - Include a disclaimer stating the product is not endorsed by Garmin and is for training purposes.

### Required Notices
- **Trademark Notice:**
  - Garmin® is a registered trademark of Garmin International Inc.
  - G1000® is a trademark of Garmin International Inc.
- **Copyright Notice:**
  - © [Year] Your Company Name. All rights reserved.

## Application to Project Documentation

Ensure the following files include appropriate disclaimers:
- `docs/G1000_simulator_documentation.md`
- `docs/G1000_simulator_roadmap.md`
- `docs/G1000_SIMULATOR_DOCUMENTATION_PLAN.md`
- `apps/flight-planner/backend/config/g1000_configuration.yaml`
- `apps/g1000-simulator/README.md`
