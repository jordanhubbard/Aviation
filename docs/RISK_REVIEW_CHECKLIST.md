# Risk Review Checklist

## Technical Risks

- **Risk:** Potential software bugs leading to incorrect mission calculations.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Conduct thorough code reviews and unit testing.
  - **Owner:** Development Team

- **Risk:** Inadequate hardware performance affecting real-time data processing.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Optimize algorithms and conduct performance testing.
  - **Owner:** Performance Team

- **Risk:** Security vulnerabilities in the codebase.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Regular security audits and vulnerability scanning.
  - **Owner:** Security Team

- **Risk:** Data integrity issues during data transfer.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Implement data validation and encryption.
  - **Owner:** Data Management Team

## Schedule Risks

- **Risk:** Delays in obtaining regulatory approvals.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Prepare documentation in advance and engage with regulators early.
  - **Owner:** Compliance Team

- **Risk:** Resource constraints impacting development timelines.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Prioritize critical features and allocate resources accordingly.
  - **Owner:** Project Management Team

- **Risk:** Dependency on third-party services.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Develop fallback mechanisms and establish a contingency plan.
  - **Owner:** Operations Team

- **Risk:** Unforeseen technical issues.
  - **Mitigation Steps:** Maintain a buffer in the schedule and allocate time for troubleshooting.
  - **Owner:** Project Management Team
