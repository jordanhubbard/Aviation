## Garmin and G1000 Usage Guidelines

- **Prohibited Uses:** Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation with Garmin without permission.
- **Permitted Uses:** Use Garmin and G1000 names/logos only for educational/training purposes with clear disclaimers.

## Acceptable Naming and Disclaimers

- **Naming:** Avoid using "Garmin" or "G1000" in product names; use descriptive alternatives.
- **Disclaimers:** Include the following disclaimer:
  > **Disclaimer:** This product is not sponsored, endorsed, or certified by Garmin International Inc. or any of its subsidiaries. It is a training simulation and is not intended for operational use.

## Required Notices

- **Trademark Notice:**
  > Garmin International Inc. is a registered trademark of Garmin International Inc.
  > G1000 is a trademark of Garmin International Inc.
- **Copyright Notice:**
