# Training Scenario Catalog

## Overview
This document outlines the catalog of pre-recorded training scenarios for the aviation project. Each scenario is designed to provide specific training objectives, starting conditions, and expected outputs.

## Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Basic Flight Navigation
- **Objective**: Train pilots on basic navigation techniques.
- **Starting Conditions**: Clear weather, daytime, standard aircraft configuration.
- **Expected Outputs**: Successful navigation from point A to point B using VFR.

### Scenario 2: Emergency Landing
- **Objective**: Prepare pilots for emergency landing procedures.
- **Starting Conditions**: Engine failure at 5000 feet, over a rural area.
- **Expected Outputs**: Safe landing at the nearest suitable airstrip.

### Scenario 3: Instrument Flight Rules (IFR) Training
- **Objective**: Train pilots on flying under IFR conditions.
- **Starting Conditions**: Overcast weather, nighttime, IFR-equipped aircraft.
- **Expected Outputs**: Successful completion of a flight plan using IFR.

## Conclusion
This catalog serves as a foundational guide for training pilots in various scenarios. Each scenario is crafted to enhance specific skills and ensure readiness for real-world situations.
